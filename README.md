
# ONLINE COLLEGE ENROLLMENT SYSTEM WITH SCHEDULED ENROLLMENT AND BLOCK SELECTION

## Project Overview

The Online College Enrollment System is a web-based platform designed to provide an alternative to face-to-face enrollment. It allows college students to enroll online, select their preferred block, view available subjects and class schedules, and submit their enrollment information without waiting in long queues at the school.

The system assigns specific enrollment dates and times for each year level. It also manages block sizes, subject schedules, enrollment limits, and student academic records to ensure an organized and efficient enrollment process.

## Project Objectives

1. To reduce long queues during the enrollment period.
2. To provide an online enrollment process accessible to students.
3. To organize enrollment schedules by year level.
4. To allow students to choose their preferred block based on availability.
5. To prevent blocks and subjects from exceeding 50 students.
6. To check student academic records and identify incomplete information.
7. To provide a final enrollment status once all enrollment conditions are satisfied.

## Main Features

- **Scheduled Enrollment** – Assigns specific enrollment dates and times for each year level.
- **Student Registration** – Allows students to register and access their accounts.
- **Academic Classification** – Identifies students as regular or irregular based on their academic records.
- **Academic Records Management** – Stores and checks student grades and GWA.
- **Subject Selection** – Displays available subjects based on academic classification.
- **Block Selection** – Allows students to choose an available block with a maximum capacity of 50 students.
- **Class Schedule Viewing** – Displays subject schedules, meeting days, and available sections.
- **Enrollment Validation** – Checks subject prerequisites, schedule conflicts, enrollment limits, and academic requirements.
- **Enrollment Confirmation** – Generates the final enrollment status after all conditions are satisfied.

## Step-by-Step Enrollment Process

1. Set the Enrollment Schedule
2. Student Registration and Academic Classification
3. Submit Academic Records
4. Display Available Subjects and Class Schedules
5. Choose a Preferred Block
6. Select Subjects and Validate Enrollment
7. Check Academic Records and Validation
8. Final Enrollment Validation
9. Confirm Enrollment

## Enrollment Rules

- Each block has a maximum capacity of 50 students.
- Each subject must not exceed 50 confirmed enrollees.
- Students can select a maximum of 8 subjects, subject to school policy.
- Students must satisfy subject prerequisites.
- Class schedules must not conflict.
- Academic records must be checked and validated.
- Irregular students must have their academic records and subject selections reviewed when necessary.

## Enrollment Status

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| Enrolled | All enrollment conditions are satisfied and enrollment is confirmed. |
| Pending Academic Validation | Academic records or subject selections require approval. |
| Pending Requirements Verification | Submitted documents or records have not yet been verified. |
| For Correction | A document or record requires correction. |
